MELVIN & ELLEN WEDDING WEBSITE — UPDATED EXPERIENCE

Includes:
- Existing black-and-gold website layout and supplied individual portraits.
- Welcome overlay with "Open Your Invitation"; audio starts after the guest taps.
- Music pause/play control.
- Gallery slideshow with fade transitions, slow zoom, looping, arrows and dots.
- Original instrumental demo at assets/melvin-ellen-love-song.wav.
- Original personalised lyrics in Our-Promise-Lyrics.txt.

Important music note:
The included audio is an original synthesized instrumental demo, not a sung vocal recording. To use a sung version, replace assets/melvin-ellen-love-song.wav with a produced song in the same format/name, or update the audio src in index.html to the uploaded MP3 filename. Browsers require a user gesture before sound playback, which is why the welcome button starts the music.

Upload index.html, wedding.ics, the entire assets folder, and optionally Our-Promise-Lyrics.txt to the ROOT of your GitHub repository. Keep filenames and capitalization exactly the same.
